Certification Program

Build a rocket, earn your certification

The UWA Aerospace Certification Program helps members earn their Level 1 or Level 2 certification with the Tripoli Rocketry Association. Once certified, you can buy and fly high-powered rocket motors up to your certification level. The program centres on building your own rocket and successfully launching and recovering it at a scheduled WARS launch day.

Certification is open to UWA Aerospace members only. Become a member to get started.
