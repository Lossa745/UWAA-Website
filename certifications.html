Certification Program

Build a rocket, earn your certification

The UWA Aerospace Certification Program is designed to help members achieve their Level 1 and/or Level 2 Tripoli Rocketry Certification. With this certification, you are licensed to launch high-powered rockets with motors up to a specified size. The program centres on building your own rocket and successfully launching and recovering it on one of our scheduled launch days.
