Hybrid engine programme

Building our own engines

While most student teams fly commercially manufactured motors, UWA Aerospace is developing its own hybrid rocket engines. Current work covers injector and combustion chamber design, construction of the oxidiser feed system, and preparation for our first static fire.

Programme status: in development. Nothing has flown yet.

01 · What a hybrid is

How a hybrid engine works

A solid motor holds its fuel and oxidiser pre-mixed in one casting: light it and it burns to completion. A liquid engine feeds both propellants in as liquids, which adds significant plumbing complexity.

A hybrid sits between them. The fuel is a solid grain cast into the chamber; the oxidiser is stored separately as a liquid or gas and fed in through an injector. Because the two are never mixed until they meet in the chamber, the motor cannot sustain combustion on its own.

04 · Test campaigns

Tested before it flies

Before any engine is considered for a vehicle it will go through an instrumented static fire campaign, logging thrust, chamber pressure and feed pressures on every fire. Building that test capability is the programme's current focus.
